Rwanda - published guidance for officials deciding immigration, nationality and refugee cases
Checked 2026-11-15. Re-run: python3 download_rw.py (from the folder above this one).

Result in one paragraph
Rwanda publishes no guidance for its decision-makers. The Directorate General of Immigration
and Emigration (DGIE) lists laws and orders on its site and has a page called "SOPs", but that
page holds only a personal-data notice for persons relocated under the UK-Rwanda partnership.
The working rules are in ministerial orders published in the Official Gazette, three of which
were saved as procedural acts: the 2019 order on immigration and emigration (visa and
residence permit classes, requirements and procedure, 104 pages), the 2022 order on Rwandan
nationality (application procedure), and the 2022 occupations-in-demand list used for work
permits. The refugee status determination committee's order could not be obtained (refugee
ministry robots.txt excludes CCBot; Refworld refuses scripts).

What is in this folder (index.tsv)
- procedural act, official (3), all trilingual Official Gazette texts (Kinyarwanda, English,
  French), titles given in English:
    Ministerial Order N°06/01 of 29/05/2019 relating to Immigration and Emigration;
    Ministerial Order Nº 007/01 of 23/03/2022 relating to Rwandan nationality;
    Annex III to Ministerial Order Nº 02/MIFOTRA/22 of 30/08/2022, occupations in demand list.
No item of kind "guidance". 3 PDF files, 2.2 MB.
